# An anti-trap measure carried into another experiment must re-earn its place

Lane L4b, 2026-08-30. Both sessions of the pair hit this in consecutive rounds,
independently, and neither spotted it in their own work.

The building session's decay fixture zeroed the first **6 ms** after the arrival.
That existed to defeat trap #18 — a fixture symmetric in time makes distinct
structures produce identical numbers, so the single-slope fixture needed a gap
between direct sound and tail. Carried unchanged into a **two-slope** fixture
whose early decay was `Te = 0.25 s`, those 6 ms of silence removed part of
exactly the region EDT reads. Measured, same fixture otherwise:

    with the 6 ms gap    EDT +17.2 %
    without it           EDT  +0.4 %

The reviewing session had carried a **20 ms pre-origin window** from lane L4a's
G3 work into the same experiment, for reasons that no longer applied either.

Neither measure was wrong where it was born. Both stopped meaning anything when
the experiment around them changed, and both survived because nobody asked the
question — the measures looked like rigour, so they read as safe.

**The rule: a guard, a gap, a window or a margin brought in from another
experiment must state, at the new site, what it is defending against THERE.**
If that sentence cannot be written, the measure is decoration and should be
removed rather than kept "to be safe".

The corollary is the sharper half. This was found only because two sessions
built two fixtures and got different answers. **Had the pair shared one fixture,
both would have been wrong and both would have been confident** — the disagreement
was the entire detection mechanism. See
[[two-builds-disagreeing-is-not-evidence-one-is-wrong]].
